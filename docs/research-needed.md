# Hisaab — "Needed" evidence (research, 4 Nov 2026 ~21:15 IST)

Scope: evidence that (a) Indian shielded-ZEC earners have a real, specific record-keeping obligation, (b) mainstream crypto-tax tools cannot see shielded ZEC, (c) the Zcash community itself documents the "viewing key → accountant-ready ledger" gap, (d) what already exists so we position honestly.
Every bullet has a source URL. "UNVERIFIED" = claim seen only in secondary/commercial sources or not confirmed on an official page. No quotes are invented; verbatim text is in quotation marks as extracted from the cited page.

## 1. India: tax + record-keeping rules for crypto (VDA)

- **Flat 30% on VDA transfer income (Sec 115BBH, Income-tax Act 1961; carried into the Income-tax Act 2025 for events from 1 Apr 2026).** Only cost of acquisition is deductible; no set-off/carry-forward of losses. Source (practitioner): https://www.taxbuddy.com/blog/section-115bbh-crypto-tax ; new-Act renumbering NOT confirmed on an official page (both taxguru and Patron Accounting say "corresponding section" without the number — UNVERIFIED which section number applies post-Apr-2026): https://taxguru.in/?p=1081077 , https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/crypto-vda-taxation-income-tax-act-2025-rules
- **1% TDS on VDA transfers (Sec 194S; TokenTax says it sits at Sec 393(1) in the 2025 Act — UNVERIFIED).** Shows in Form 26AS/AIS; mismatches with your own records invite scrutiny. https://tokentax.co/blog/guide-to-crypto-taxes-in-india ; https://www.incorpx.io/blog/crypto-vda-tax-rules-tds-capital-gains-india
- **Schedule VDA (ITR-2, ITR-3, ITR-5, ITR-6, ITR-7) is transaction-wise.** Official Income Tax Dept page confirms applicability to those forms: https://incometaxindia.gov.in/w/schedule_vda (page returned 403 to our fetcher; applicability confirmed via search snippet — field list below is from practitioner pages).
- **Per-transfer fields a taxpayer must report:** serial no., **date of acquisition**, **date of transfer**, head of income (capital gains / business), **cost of acquisition (INR)**, **consideration received (INR)**; loss shown as nil. "requires taxpayers to furnish detailed transaction-wise information for every transfer of VDAs during the financial year." https://www.taxheal.com/?p=129471 ; same list + TDS-under-194S column: https://www.taxscan.in/top-stories/how-to-report-crypto-nfts-tokens-income-schedule-vda-in-itr-explained-1448305 ; KoinX's Schedule VDA guide: https://www.koinx.com/tax-guides/file-crypto-taxes-on-schedule-vda
- **Records a CA will ask for (practitioner checklist, CA Sundaram Gupta, 6 Apr 2026):** per-platform history with timestamps, quantities, **INR price at the time**, transaction type; receipts as proof of cost; INR value on date received for income; Form 26AS/AIS. https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/crypto-vda-taxation-income-tax-act-2025-rules
  → For a freelancer paid in shielded ZEC, every one of those fields lives inside encrypted notes/memos; nothing on the public chain gives the CA any of it. That is the record Hisaab produces.
- **Financial year = 1 Apr–31 Mar** (Schedule VDA is per FY) — Zodl/Zashi's tax export is per **calendar** year (see §3), so it does not even line up with the Indian FY.
- **2025–2026 changes (keep short):**
  - Income-tax Act 2025: assent 21 Aug 2025, in force **1 Apr 2026**; "crypto-asset" explicitly added to the VDA definition from 1 Apr 2026. https://taxguru.in/?p=1081077
  - **Sec 509(1), Income-tax Act 2025:** designated reporting entities (exchanges/wallet providers) must file crypto-asset transaction statements (India's domestic CARF plumbing); applies from 1 Apr 2026. https://taxguru.in/?p=1081077 ; draft rules 241–244 for the regime: https://a2ztaxcorp.net/?p=26059
  - **Budget 2026 (1 Feb 2026):** penalties for reporting entities — ₹200/day for non-filing, ₹50,000 for inaccurate info (Sec 446). Sitharaman: "it is proposed to introduce a penalty provision." https://www.oneindia.com/business/didn-t-declare-crypto-assets-users-are-on-the-tax-radar-after-budget-2026-7984943.html ; https://www.analyticsinsight.net/amp/story/news/budget-2026-tightens-rules-on-crypto-reporting-what-investors-need-to-know
  - **CARF:** India committed to implement from **April 2027** (KoinX, taxguru) — UNVERIFIED against an official MoF statement; an older Bloomberg Law piece said India had not yet formally committed. https://www.koinx.com/in/crypto-tax-guide ; https://news.bloomberglaw.com/business-and-practice/companies-using-cryptoassets-must-prepare-for-indias-tax-shift
  - Under-reporting penalty for individuals: Sec 270A, 50%–200% of tax on under-reported income. https://taxguru.in/?p=1081077
  → Positioning line: reporting pressure is moving onto intermediaries + CARF; a self-custodied shielded earner has **no intermediary producing a statement for them** — they must produce their own, and currently can't.

## 2. Do mainstream crypto-tax tools see shielded Zcash? (No — transparent-only or manual CSV)

- **CoinTracker (official page, verbatim):** "Paste your Zcash t-address (starts with t1 or t3) into the search bar. Shielded addresses (z-addresses) are not publicly viewable." … "Enter a Zcash t-address into CoinTracker to view real-time balances and public transactions. Shielded transactions remain private." … "Shielded transactions are not publicly accessible." https://www.cointracker.io/wallet/zcash (its integrations page also says download a CSV and reformat to CoinTracker's template: https://www.cointracker.io/integrations/zcash — UNVERIFIED, 403 to our fetcher)
- **Koinly (official page):** import = "via your public address or CSV file upload"; "We recommend syncing your XPUB key rather than individual addresses." No mention of z-/unified addresses or viewing keys anywhere on the page. https://koinly.io/integrations/zcash/
- **KoinX (India):** no Zcash integration page found on koinx.com; release notes 2026 (ZebPay, Coinbase, Hyperliquid) don't mention Zcash; KoinX adds wallets "through public addresses". UNVERIFIED that Zcash is supported at all. https://www.koinx.com/releases/2026/april ; https://www.koinx.com/in/crypto-tax-guide
- **CoinLedger, ZenLedger, TaxNodes:** no help-centre page found in the time box stating shielded support either way — UNVERIFIED; do not claim.
- **Fireblocks (custody, for context):** supports t-address ZEC only, "no current plans to add shielded address support". https://support.fireblocks.io/hc/en-us/articles/26026114047900-Zcash-ZEC-transactions-on-Fireblocks
- **Independent write-up (BloFin Academy, 29 Aug 2026):** "Paste a shielded Zcash address into a tax tool and you will get back an empty history. Not an error and not a warning, simply nothing at all, because there is nothing published on the chain for the tool to read." … "One tax tool's own Zcash integration page documents its import route as syncing all transactions from a public address, which is a transparent address, with a self-prepared spreadsheet upload as the alternative." … on export: "There is no single documented answer right now, which is the honest position." https://blofin.com/academy/education/zcash/zcash-tax-reporting-with-shielded-funds (AI-assisted editorial; use as corroboration, not primary)

## 3. Zcash community evidence of the gap (forum, ECC/Zodl, ZecHub, wallets)

- **Forum, "Is anyone actually using viewing keys for business accounting?" (ZKZeek, 20 Jun 2026):** "Viewing keys are supposed to be the answer here." … "The bookkeeper needs records and the auditor needs proof, and tax season doesn't care that your chain is private." … "But I can't find much evidence that anyone uses them this way." — notes nothing turns a viewing key into "an exportable ledger or a clean income report." https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/is-anyone-actually-using-viewing-keys-for-business-accounting/56300
  - Reply Michae2xl (20 Jun): "The encryption is done; the boring middle is not."
  - Reply vampirot (21 Jun): "even the access to viewing keys is not exactly easy (gotta really go digging)…"
  - Reply aryaethn (13 Jul): bookkeeper needs an FVK (not IVK) so change isn't counted as income; "what's missing is the accounting semantics on top."
  - Reply dismad (ZecHub): "We are actively supporting builders for exactly this type of much needed tooling in our current hackathon!"
  - zooko (23 Jun) confirmed viewing keys are NOT being discontinued ("No.").
- **Forum, "Exporting transaction history to JSON/CSV from UFVK/seed" (snacks, 10 Feb 2026):** Zashi's "tax" export "contains almost no information, not even txids"; Ywallet/Zkool/Zingo "all have really terrible export options - nowhere close to the information you get from zcashd"; wanted fields: "txid, datetime, kind (send/receive, memo etc), fee, any notes connected, to/from addresses". Replies: zingo-cli supports viewkeys (dismad), Zkool supports view keys + export and has a GraphQL `transactionsByAccount` API (hanh), Zenith imports UFVKs (pitmutt), dismad's exportToJSON script. https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/exporting-transaction-history-to-json-csv-from-ufvk-seed/54662
- **Forum, "Shielded Viewing Keys" (zecfi, 28 Feb 2026):** a treasury holder with a UVK could not verify/audit its own shielded balances; "the only keys I see are unified viewing key or view key (private)"; asked for "an easy 1.2.3. step guide for holders"; daviddumont: tools may expect a Sapling IVK "not the unified wrapper"; Autotunafish: after importing a viewing key "you have to rescan the wallet." https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/shielded-viewing-keys/54837
- **Zodl/Zashi "Export Tax File" (official support article):** CSV columns are only Date, Received Quantity/Currency, Sent Quantity/Currency, Fee Amount/Currency, Tag — **no txid, no memo, no address, no fiat value**; covers only the **previous calendar year** (not Indian FY); warns the file is "_unencrypted_ data about your transactions." https://support.zodl.com/article/22-exporting-a-tax-file ; feature shipped in v1.4 (3 Mar 2025) per changelog mirror: https://gitea.interbiznw.com/zec/secant-android-wallet/src/branch/rust-zcs-0.17/docs/whatsNew/WHATS_NEW_EN.md
- **ECC/Zodl, "Selective Disclosure & Shielded Viewing Keys" (22 Jan 2018):** the canonical use case is a business owner who can "disclose their payment address's viewing key to their accountant for keeping track of all revenue". https://zodl.com/viewing-keys-selective-disclosure (301 from https://electriccoin.co/blog/viewing-keys-selective-disclosure) ; ECC "Explaining viewing keys" (merchant→accountant example; Sapling viewing keys show value, memo, target address): https://electriccoin.co/blog/explaining-viewing-keys ; ECC memo post: a view-key holder "would be able to see the memo, along with the amount and the recipient address of the transaction" https://electriccoin.co/blog/encrypted-memo-field/
- **ZecHub wiki, Viewing Keys:** "A viewing key is the tool for selective disclosure." … "prefer the narrowest key that answers the question being asked." … "a handed-out key cannot be recalled — only outlived by moving the funds." … "every extra holder of a key is a permanent privacy credit line you cannot close." Auditor/proof-of-reserves and exchange-deposit-detection use cases; Zallet imports Sapling EFVKs only, unified `uview…` keys need Zkool. https://zechub.wiki/zcash-tech/viewing-keys

## 4. What already exists (position honestly)

- **ZecLedger (vancube, Aug–Sep 2026)** — closest prior art. "read-only shielded accounting tool for Zcash (CLI + a public no-keys web dashboard)"; input = UFVK; outputs balances, tx history, cost-basis reports, "payment reconciliation", privacy checks; can display memos; local processing. Asked $10k retroactive (FPF Coinholder-Directed Q3) → **Rejected – "Do Not Support the Proposed Project"** (29 Sep 2026). Author: "still a project under active development"; independent mainnet testing "uncovered issues"; hanh: website "feels 100% AI generated". No invoice-code matching, no India/INR, no FY, no accountant hand-off format, no per-payment verifiable receipt described. https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/retroactive-grant-zecledger/56969 ; https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/zecledger-grant-update/57762 ; results: https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/q3-coinholder-directed-retroactive-grants-program-results/57909 ; repo https://github.com/vancube2/zecledger
- **ZecBooks / SaneBooks (sane-apps, v0.1.1, MIT)** — macOS-only app (+ CLI) that imports a UFVK, classifies rows income/expense/change/fee, exports CSV/PDF and an encrypted expiring ".sanebooks" proof pack for a read-only accountant Reader. "Fiat marks remain user/import supplied; ZecBooks does not currently claim a live price-source integration." Not tax software; no memo/invoice matching mentioned; 0 stars/0 forks at fetch time. https://github.com/sane-apps/SaneBooks
- **CipherPay (Kenbak)** — merchant checkout: paste a viewing key, trial-decrypts, dashboard with "Amounts, status, memos, CSV export"; funded in Q3 2026 retro grants. It is a payment/checkout product, not a tax ledger. https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/is-anyone-actually-using-viewing-keys-for-business-accounting/56300 ; https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/q3-coinholder-directed-retroactive-grants-program-results/57909
- **ZGo (pitmutt)** — payment app: "export feature that provides every order, the ZEC amount and the ZEC price at the time for accounting purposes" — order-side (merchant creates the order in ZGo), not viewing-key-side. Same thread.
- **Zkool** (hhanh00) — watch-only accounts from unified keys + GraphQL `transactionsByAccount` export; raw data, no accounting/tax semantics. https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/exporting-transaction-history-to-json-csv-from-ufvk-seed/54662 ; https://github.com/hhanh00/zkool2/wiki
- **zingo-cli + dismad's exportToJSON script**, **Zenith** (UFVK import, JSON via RPC), **zcash-devtool**, **Zallet** (Sapling EFVK only, beta) — developer-grade exports, no ledger/receipts. Same thread + https://zechub.wiki/zcash-tech/viewing-keys
- **CipherScan** — client-side decryption of a single tx given viewing key (+ wallet birthday); per-tx view, not a ledger. https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/shielded-viewing-keys/54837
- **Zodl/Zashi Export Tax File** — 8 columns, calendar-year only, no txid/memo/fiat (see §3).

**Honest one-liner:** viewing-key → CSV already exists in developer form (Zkool, zingo-cli, Zenith) and two early, unfunded/unreviewed bookkeeping attempts (ZecLedger — rejected by coinholders Sep 2026; ZecBooks — v0.1.1 macOS, no fiat pricing). None does invoice-code memo matching, Indian FY (Apr–Mar) Schedule-VDA-shaped output with INR consideration at receipt, or verifiable per-payment receipts that let the CA check a single payment without the key.
